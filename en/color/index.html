Choose from basic to premium levels for your color. We offer root (2 cm) and full color, henna, hair manicure, and root, full and design bleach, plus a separate men's color and bleach menu.

All four of our designers have completed L'Oreal color training. For care with your color, see Hair Treatments.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a neat root touch-up
  • Those who want to choose between basic and premium color
  • Anyone looking for henna or a hair manicure
  • Those preparing for a bright color with bleach

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is root color?
Basic root color (2 cm) is ₩60,000 and premium root color (2 cm) is ₩70,000. Men's basic root color (2 cm) is ₩50,000.
How much is bleach?
Bleach is ₩80,000 for the roots, ₩90,000 for the full head and ₩100,000 for design bleach. Men's bleach is ₩70,000 for the roots and ₩80,000 for the full head.
Can I add partial color to a men's haircut?
Yes. Adding partial color (sideburns) to a men's cut adds ₩10,000–₩20,000 to the cut price.
