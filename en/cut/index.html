From tassel cuts to hush cuts,
haircuts in Jiwell City

Men's cuts and women's basic cuts are priced by stylist level: head director, store manager and designer. We also list design cuts, scalp scaling cuts, kids' cuts, bangs trims, and blow-dry and shampoo menus separately.

Designer Jiyeon specializes in tassel and layered cuts, Director Myeongju Oh in dandy, layered and volume cuts, Store Manager Sangnim Lee in hush cuts, and Head Director Geumsuk Son in hush and layered cuts. If you want volume too, see perms and volume straightening in Jiwell City.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a design cut such as a tassel, layered or hush cut
  • Men looking for a dandy cut or volume cut
  • Kids, or anyone who just wants a quick bangs trim
  • Those who want a blow-dry or shampoo with their cut

Haircut FAQ

How much is a men's haircut?
A men's cut is ₩30,000 with the head director, ₩22,000 with the store manager and ₩20,000 with a designer.
How much is a women's haircut?
A women's basic cut is ₩30,000 with the head director and ₩25,000 with a designer, and a design cut is ₩27,000.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes. A kids' cut is ₩20,000 and a kids' bangs trim is ₩3,000. An adult bangs trim is ₩5,000.
