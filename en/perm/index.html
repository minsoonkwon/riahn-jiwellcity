From volume straightening to
setting and repair perms

Choose from basic to premium levels for your perm. Basic perms are priced for the head director, designers, teens and kids, and basic heat perms (volume straightening, setting, straightening + setting) for the head director, designers and teens. Men's basic perms, heat perms and down perms have their own menu.

Designer Jiyeon specializes in volume straightening and down perms, Store Manager Sangnim Lee in volume straightening, S-curl perms and heat perms, and Head Director Geumsuk Son in setting perms and straightening. For care alongside your perm, see Hair Treatments.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting smoother, calmer hair with volume straightening or straightening
  • Those who want natural curls with a setting perm or S-curl perm
  • Men who want a down perm to tidy the sides
  • Families looking for teen and kids' perm prices

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
Basic heat perm volume straightening is ₩130,000 with the head director or a designer and ₩120,000 for teens; premium volume straightening is ₩140,000. Men's volume straightening is ₩110,000 (basic) and ₩120,000 (premium).
How much is a men's down perm?
A men's down perm is ₩30,000 for the sides and ₩50,000 for the full head. Volume straightening + down perm is ₩130,000 (basic) and ₩150,000 (premium).
Do you have perm prices for teens and kids?
Yes. A basic perm is ₩70,000 for teens and ₩60,000 for kids, and a premium perm is ₩90,000 for teens. Basic heat perm volume straightening, setting and straightening + setting are ₩120,000, ₩130,000 and ₩140,000 for teens.
