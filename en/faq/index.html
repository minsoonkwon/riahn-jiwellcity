FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book your date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-43-252-2044) or Instagram DM.
Is parking available?
Yes. Parking is free.
What are your hours?
We are open 10:00–19:00 on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.
When are you closed?
We are closed every Tuesday, as well as on Seollal and Chuseok (Korean holidays) and the day after each.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩3,000–₩39,000 (including bangs trims), perms and straightening ₩20,000–₩230,000, color ₩50,000–₩100,000 and treatments ₩50,000–₩100,000. Blow-dry and shampoo are ₩8,000–₩30,000.
Do you have prices for teens and kids?
Yes. A kids' cut is ₩20,000, and a basic perm is ₩70,000 for teens and ₩60,000 for kids. Basic heat perm volume straightening is ₩120,000 for teens.
Do prices differ by stylist?
Yes. Some cut, perm and blow-dry menus are priced by stylist level: head director, store manager and designer. For example, a men's cut is ₩30,000 with the head director, ₩22,000 with the store manager and ₩20,000 with a designer.
Do you have a membership?
Our prepaid membership adds 15% bonus credit on ₩300,000 and 20% on ₩500,000, and family members or friends can share it with you.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your designer when you book. Each designer's specialties are on the Our Designers page.
How do I find the salon?
From Jiwell City Mall 1, go straight toward Solbat Elementary School. We are on the 3rd floor of the building with Tous Les Jours (LK Triplex, Building A).

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (10:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays).
