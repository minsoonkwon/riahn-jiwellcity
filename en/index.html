About

From cuts to treatments,
RIAHN Hair Cheongju Jiwell City

We specialize in cuts, perms, color and hair treatments for both men and women. Prices are set by stylist level (head director, designer) and for teens and kids, so the whole family can visit together.

Our prepaid membership gives you bonus credit, and family members or friends can share it with you.

Opened in 2026 with 9 styling chairs, we are on the 3rd floor of Building A, LK Triplex, 39 Daenong-ro, Heungdeok-gu, Cheongju-si, Chungcheongbuk-do — the Tous Les Jours building toward Solbat Elementary School from Jiwell City Mall 1. Free parking is available.
