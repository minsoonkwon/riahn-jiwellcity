FAQ

Treatment FAQ

What treatments do you offer?
Single treatments are ₩50,000 (1 step) and ₩70,000 (2 steps). A 3-step full course is ₩60,000 for men and ₩80,000 for women, and a 4-step full course is ₩90,000 for men and ₩100,000 for women.
Does the price depend on hair length?
Prices by hair length are ₩100,000 for short hair, then +₩10,000 for bob to shoulder length, +₩20,000 for chest length and +₩30,000 for hair below the chest.
Can I choose based on my hair condition?
Yes. You can choose anything from a single treatment (1–2 steps) to a full course (3–4 steps) to suit your hair condition.
